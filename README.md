# Recital

A public-domain poem a day, read aloud by LibriVox volunteers, and a small example of
designing a Flutter app for iPhone Duo: bars in the side column, two panes on the inner
display, and nothing interactive in the fold. `docs/reference/iphone-duo.md` collects
Apple's guidance with its sources; `docs/decisions/DEC-3.md` says how the app applies it.

The poems' texts come from public-domain editions on Wikisource and Project Gutenberg, and
the readings from LibriVox, whose recordings are in the public domain; `assets/catalog.json`
gives the source of each.
